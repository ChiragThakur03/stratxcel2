# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are small-to-midsize business leaders, startup founders, and strategic decision-makers who require high-impact, accessible market intelligence, competitive analysis, and strategic guidance without the cost or friction of traditional management consulting firms.

## Product Purpose

StratxceL democratizes strategic business intelligence by uniting AI-driven analytical tools with expert human consulting. It empowers organizations to rapidly evaluate market trends, forecast opportunities, monitor performance KPIs, and book strategic consulting sessions to achieve business growth. Success means enabling clients to make confident, data-backed strategic decisions faster and more affordably.

## Positioning

StratxceL is a hybrid AI + Human Expert Consulting platform. Unlike standalone AI analytics tools or traditional slow-moving consulting agencies, StratxceL pairs automated real-time strategic intelligence with direct access to seasoned business advisors.

## Operating Context

- Web application accessible on desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.
- Workflows include executive dashboard monitoring, real-time competitive analysis, automated KPI tracking, plan selection/upgrade, and direct expert consultation booking.
- Built as a React 18 single-page application with TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion animations, and Spline 3D interactive visuals.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **3-Tier Subscription Model**: Starter ($99/mo), Professional ($299/mo), Enterprise ($999/mo).
- **AI Business Analytics**: Automated market intelligence, KPI monitoring, and predictive forecasting.
- **Expert Consultancy**: Dedicated advisory booking workflow for specialized human consulting.
- **White-label Reporting**: Custom branded reporting capabilities for client presentations.
- **Technical Stack**: React 18, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, GSAP, Spline 3D (`@splinetool/react-spline`).

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: StratxceL
- **Tone & Voice**: Authoritative, forward-looking, professional, empowering, and innovative.
- **Identity & Visuals**: Rich dark-mode aesthetic, vibrant purple/indigo gradients, smooth micro-animations, interactive 3D elements (Spline), and clean modern typography.

## Product Principles

1. **Hybrid Intelligence**: Combine automated AI speed and scale with human strategic depth.
2. **Accessible Excellence**: Make tier-one strategic consulting accessible to growth-stage businesses.
3. **Clarity & Actionability**: Deliver clear, scannable data and actionable insights over complex jargon.
4. **Immersive & Premium Experience**: Maintain a high-craft visual identity that inspires trust and authority.
